    Limitations of this evidence

    • App-store and Play reviews skew to complaints; Reddit skews to Android power users.
    • The Reddit sample covers a 2026-03-19 → 2026-10-06 archive window (rate-limit deviation, stated on the deck).
    • Gates are model-inferred. Human agreement on the held-out test set (n=40, prompt v1): is_retrieval 97.5%, photo_type 100%, failure_gate 67.5% (κ=0.569), outcome 80.0%.
    • Results show failure types in a convenience sample — not prevalence in the Google Photos population.
    • 182 of 765 screened relevant documents became extraction episodes; only 3 scorecard cells reach n≥8.
