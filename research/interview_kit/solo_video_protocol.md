# Solo think-aloud probe — video protocol (for the 6 friend recordings)

You (the friend) will record **your own phone screen + your voice** for about **10 minutes**.
You are playing the participant. No interviewer, no preparation. This is the real-user
evidence for the project. Keep everything private — only your search process is on camera
(you can look away when you open the actual photo if you like).

## Before you start (say it to the camera, 30 seconds)
1. Your first name only (or a nickname).
2. "I have used Google Photos for about ___ years."
3. "I have roughly ___ photos in it." (hundreds / a few thousand / 10,000+)
4. "My phone is an Android / iPhone. Backup is automatic: yes / no."
5. "One photo I have photographed for a reason — a medicine, a ticket, notes, a receipt,
   a screenshot — is: ___" (just name the kind, 10 seconds)

## PART 1 — The last time you searched (60 seconds, no screen needed)
Tell the camera: the last time you tried to find an old photo/screenshot and had trouble.
What were you looking for? What did you type? What happened? Did you find it?
(If you never had trouble, just say so and skip to Part 2.)

## PART 2 — The probe (5 minutes HARD STOP)
1. **Choose a photo from at least 2 years ago** that you remember reasonably well but not
   perfectly. (A trip, a festival, a place, a person. Anything.)
2. **Do NOT open Google Photos yet.** Describe it to the camera like you're explaining it
   to a friend: say everything you remember, and for each thing say whether you are
   **sure** or you **just think so**. (e.g. "sure it was Hampi", "I just think it was evening")
3. **Now open Google Photos and find it. THINK OUT LOUD** — say what you type, why,
   and what you see. Start a mental stopwatch at your first tap.
   - At **5 minutes, STOP** even if you are not done. Say: "I'm stopping."
   - If you find it: say "Found it" and show ONLY the results screen (you can hide the
     photo itself).
4. **The reveal (60 seconds):** look at the actual photo (on camera if comfortable).
   Say: what did you forget? what did you remember wrongly?
5. **Two scores, out loud:**
   - "Before I started, I was ___ out of 5 sure I'd find it."
   - "How easy was it, 1 to 7 — ___."
6. **Last 20 seconds:** "When search fails, I usually ___" (what do you actually do —
   ask someone? scroll? check Instagram?)

## After the video (2 minutes, on WhatsApp)
Send the video + this one-line summary:
`Nickname | Android/iPhone | yrs | photos | probe: found/not/partial | time found | quote`

**Do not:** show anyone else's face or name, show documents with personal details
(cover the screen), or paste the video anywhere except the private group.
The video is deleted after the project.
