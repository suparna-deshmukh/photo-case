# R1 Item 9 — Recruiting plan (6 participants in 24 h)

**Where (Goa campus):**
1. Own hostel / flat groups — 2–3 candidates (fastest).
2. Batch WhatsApp groups (2–3 groups) — send the recruitment message in the evening (6–8 PM).
3. Club groups (photography/tech/cultural) — 1–2 candidates.

**Target mix:**
- 4–5 target segment: 3+ years on Google Photos, 2,000+ photos, Android, had search trouble in last 3 months.
- 1–2 boundary-check: lighter users (1–3 yrs) or iPhone (max 2 total boundary).

**Rules:**
- At most ONE friend (close) per three participants; log `relationship` per participant (friend/acquaintance/stranger) — this is a stated limitation.
- First-come-first-served within the screener rules; overbook by 2 to absorb no-shows (8 slots, 6 interviews).
- Thank-you: ₹100 (Swiggy/e-mojo) after the call.

**Timeline (24 h):**
- T+0h: send message in groups, DM 3 known-likely candidates.
- T+4h: chase form non-responders once ("just checking, no pressure").
- T+8h: schedule all 6–8 into slots; confirm each by DM 2 h before.
- T+24h: all interviews done, Note Sheet rows sent.
