# R1 Item 8 — Logistics

- **Call:** one recurring Google Meet link (BITS account), shared only with scheduled participants.
- **Screen-share check (5 min before):** participant shares phone screen; interviewer confirms they can see it, can pause sharing, and can look away from photo contents.
- **Recording:** phone voice-memo only with consent (Meet recording is not used). Delete recordings after the project.
- **Notes:** live in the Note Sheet (or paper, transcribed within 10 minutes).
- **Coded within 10 minutes** of each interview (cue rows, gates, quotes).
- **Privacy:** no names in any note; participant = P-code; recordings deleted post-project;
  data deleted on request.
