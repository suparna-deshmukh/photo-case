# R1 Item 3 — Consent script (spoken, < 90 seconds)

"Hi, thanks for your time. Before we start, a quick word about the study. I'm studying how people
find old photos they've taken, as part of a student research project — not a product test and not
connected to Google in any way. Everything is voluntary: you can skip any question, and you can stop
the interview at any time by just saying so. In my report you'll appear only as a code like P3 — no
name, no phone number, nothing that identifies you.

I may record the audio on my phone only so I don't miss details — that's completely up to you, and
the recording is deleted after the project. You can also decline to show any photo itself: in the
screen-sharing part, you only need to show the searching, not the pictures. And anything you share,
if you ask, I will delete on request.

Do I have your okay to begin?"

(If yes: "Great. First the short form answers we discussed…" If no: do not proceed.)
