# R1 Item 1 — Screener (Google Form)

Create a Google Form (BITS account), keep responses private to the owner, share the link only inside
recruitment message. Title: "Photo search study — 1 min form".
Description line: "Voluntary, anonymous in my report, you can stop anytime."

## Questions (in this order)

**Q1. Do you use Google Photos on your phone?**  *(single choice; required)*
- Yes, always  ← accept
- Sometimes    ← accept
- No           ← disqualify (form ends, thanks + no further questions)

**Q2. Since roughly when have you been backing up photos to Google Photos?**  *(required)*
- Less than 1 year
- 1–3 years
- 3+ years     ← target segment (prefer)

Acceptance rule: prefer 3+ years; allow 1–3 years for **at most 2** boundary-check interviews.

**Q3. About how many photos and videos do you have in Google Photos?**  *(required)*
- Less than 2,000
- 2,000–10,000  ← prefer
- 10,000+       ← prefer
- Not sure

**Q4. Which type of phone do you mainly use for photos?**  *(required)*
- Android  ← prefer
- iPhone

**Q5. In the last 3 months, have you tried to find an old photo, video or screenshot and had trouble?**  *(required)*
- Yes  ← prefer
- No

**Q6. Do you have 25 minutes in the next 24 hours for a video call?**  *(required, time slots)*
- Today 6–9 PM IST
- Today 9–11 PM IST
- Tomorrow 11 AM–2 PM IST
- Tomorrow 4–7 PM IST
- Tomorrow 7–10 PM IST
- None of these

**Q7. Your first name or a nickname (for scheduling only), and a WhatsApp number or email.**  *(short answer ×2; required)*
Note under the field: "Used only for scheduling. Your responses stay private to me and you appear anonymously (P1…P6) in any report."

## Scoring (for the researcher only — do not put on the form)
- Target segment: Q2 = 3+ yrs, Q3 = 2,000+, Q4 = Android, Q5 = Yes.
- Boundary-check (max 2 slots): Q2 = 1–3 yrs or Q4 = iPhone or Q3 < 2,000.
- Disqualify: Q1 = No.
