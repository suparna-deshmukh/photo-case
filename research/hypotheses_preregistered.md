H1: Most retrieval failures happen at refinement and evaluation, not interpretation.
H2: People reliably remember context (event, people, purpose, before/after relations) more than content labels or exact dates; recalled dates are often wrong by months.
H3: After a failed query, users change keywords at random instead of adding discriminating information, and many fall back to scrolling the timeline.
H4: Utility photos (medicine, notes, receipts) are remembered by purpose and text; experience photos are remembered by relational context.
H5: After two failed queries, success probability drops sharply; users either scroll or stop searching.
