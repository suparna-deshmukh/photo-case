# The 3 most common retrieval scenarios (simulated; task cards for deck + future M5)

From the 6 simulated transcripts (protocol-validation study). Real friend probe videos
will replace/validate these.

## S1 — "The document I photographed for a reason" (P2, P3, P4, P5 — 4/6)
- **Remembered:** what it shows (ticket/medicine/notes/timetable), WHY it was taken
  (purpose), rough season or event.
- **Forgotten:** the exact date, the exact text in it, which phone/album.
- **Tried:** single keyword ("medicine", "whiteboard", "timetable") → either instant win
  or a pile of near-identical documents; screenshot text often not found by keyword.
- **Why it breaks:** documents look alike at thumbnail scale and the discriminating
  detail (the text) is exactly what search can't read well.
- **Opportunity:** text-in-image + purpose cues as first-class search signals;
  "I photographed this because…" as an input.

## S2 — "The trip photo that has a twin" (P1, P3, P6 — 3/6)
- **Remembered:** place (often wrong city among similar trips), one distinctive visual
  (boat, lights, stage), the feeling.
- **Forgotten:** WHICH of two similar trips/years; the distinguishing detail was never
  encoded in memory.
- **Tried:** place keywords ("hampi boat", "boat sunset", "gokarna"/"goa") → near-
  duplicates; wrong pick then date-check; reformulations swap keywords, add no new cue.
- **Why it breaks:** near-duplicate set + the user's memory has no discriminating cue;
  results are visually too similar to compare at a glance.
- **Opportunity:** side-by-side comparison of near-duplicates with dates/places shown
  large; ask the ONE discriminating question ("which year? which trip?").

## S3 — "The group shot I can't pin to a night" (P1, P6 — 2/6)
- **Remembered:** who was there (often wrong on the exact roster), the event (fest week),
  the vibe (lights, crowd).
- **Forgotten:** which exact night/week, who else was in frame.
- **Tried:** "fest", "corridor", "fest group" → many similar group photos; fallback =
  ask a friend to resend (WhatsApp) or give up.
- **Why it breaks:** event-level memory is coarse (a fest = many similar nights);
  people cues are partly wrong, so face search alone can't disambiguate.
- **Opportunity:** event-context narrowing (festival week → day) + people set refinement
  ("with Rohan but not with Aman").
